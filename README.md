_Suite à la découverte de [plusieurs failles majeures](https://www.lemonde.fr/pixels/article/2026/10/22/failles-php-pourquoi-ces-vulnerabilites-perturbent-votre-navigation-sur-internet_6798629_4408996.html) au sein du langage PHP, nous avons décidé de mettre notre site hors ligne, par mesure de prudence. ## Membership
Membership of the Society is managed by Liverpool University Press. To join the Society, or to manage your membership, please email [subscriptions@liverpool.ac.uk](mailto:subscriptions@liverpool.ac.uk).

## Contact the Society
If you are already in contact with a Committee member, please continue to use that email address. Otherwise, please contact the IT Officer, Edward Mills, via [webmaster@sfs.ac.uk](mailto:webmaster@sfs.ac.uk), in the first instance, who will be happy to put you in contact with the relevant person to answer any queries.

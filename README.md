### Welcome to the (temporary) website of the Society for French Studies, the foremost learned association for French studies in the UK and Ireland.

Following the discovery of a [vulnerability](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9j34zgmkxx1o) in PHP, a widely-used component of many websites, we have temporarily taken down our regular site out of an abundance of caution. This temporary page will hold essential information about the Society's activities, and will be updated as regularly as possible. We hope to be able to bring our regular site back online within a couple of weeks.

## Global Research Forum: Friday 30th october
The Society is delighted to announce the launch of an online global research forum, allowing scholars from outside the European / US academy to present their work, exchange ideas, and network with other researchers based in the 'Global North'. We hope that this initiative might also connect the society better with colleagues beyond Europe and the US, strengthening the society's presence globally. Our inaugural seminar willtake place on Friday 30th October 2026 at 1pm (BST), on the theme: _'Faire taire le passé, ouvrir les archives — familiales, locales, nationales'/ 'Silencing the past, opening the archive — familial, local, national'._ We are delighted to host two excellent speakers: Dr Siba Bartaki, The English and Foreign Languages University Hyderabad, will present on "La politique de l’invisibilisation du travailleur engagé indien", and Dr Angélique Ibáñez Aristondo, University College Dublin, will speak on "Violences de genre, silences et autorité narrative dans La Maison vide de Laurent Mauvignier".

Papers will last 20 minutes each, allowing 15-20 minutes for discussion; we will wrap up by 2pm BST. To join, use [this link](https://www.sfs.ac.uk/about-us/meet.google.com/chx-vsjt-brn).

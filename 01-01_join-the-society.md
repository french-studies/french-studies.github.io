## Join the Society
Members of the Society are eligible for a range of funding offered by the Society, and enjoy preferential rates when registering for our annual conference. In addition to academics from a range of different countries, our membership includes librarians, curators and postgraduate students. Membership is open to anyone who wishes to support the aims and activities of the Society. A reduced rate is available to retired members, postgraduate students, members not in full-time employment, and new lecturers in their first three years in post. The annual membership fee is currently £55 with a reduced rate of £27.50 for postgraduate students, those on temporary or casual contracts, unwaged/retired and new lecturers within 3 years of obtaining their first permanent post.

Membership of the Society is managed jointly between the Society and Liverpool University Press, in order to facilitate members receiving their copies of _French Studies: A Quarterly Review_ and _French Studies Bulletin: A Quarterly Supplement_ (included with members' subscriptions). To join the Society, or to update your details, please email subscriptions@liverpool.ac.uk, or telephone +44 (0)151 795 1080.
